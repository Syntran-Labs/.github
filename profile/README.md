# Syntran Labs

**Synthetic Translation for Engineering Systems.**

Syntran Labs explores how ideas, research, specifications, and technical decisions can be translated into working software systems through AI-assisted engineering.

## What Syntran means

Syntran comes from **Synthetic Translation**: the practice of using AI-assisted workflows to translate abstract intent into structured, reviewable, and executable engineering outputs.

That translation can happen at different levels:

- From research papers to runnable code
- From learning goals to educational projects
- From business or technical requirements to production-grade systems
- From architecture decisions to documented implementation patterns

## Repository tracks

### Systems Lab

Production-oriented systems, architecture patterns, and secure engineering case studies.

This track focuses on real-world engineering concerns such as security, traceability, auditability, access control, backend architecture, data workflows, and operational readiness.

### Learning Lab

Educational engineering projects designed to explore, explain, and teach technical concepts.

This track focuses on clarity, reproducibility, and practical learning. Projects may not be intended for production, but they are built with professional structure and documentation.

### Paper Lab

Practical explorations and implementations of research papers in AI, ML, data, and software engineering.

This track focuses on turning research ideas into simplified implementations, experiments, explanations, and engineering notes.

## Engineering principles

Syntran Labs projects are guided by a few core principles:

1. Ideas should become structured artifacts.
2. AI-assisted work should remain human-reviewable.
3. Documentation is part of the engineering output.
4. Security and privacy must be considered from the beginning.
5. Learning projects should be clear, reproducible, and honest about their scope.
6. Production-oriented projects should include architecture, trade-offs, limitations, and operational concerns.
